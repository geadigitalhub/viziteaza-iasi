About the platform

Who they are and where they come from

The platform grew out of the travel-tech ecosystem behind the flight search engine Aviasales — people who have lived and breathed tourism for over a decade. The business model

What they want and why they do it

The model is simple and honest: travel brands want customers, and you have an audience and their trust. Travelpayouts sits in the middle and "translates" between the two. When someone in your audience clicks your link and books a flight or a hotel, the brand gains a customer and you earn a commission.

Travelpayouts, in turn, keeps a share of the commission it negotiated with the brand — so it's in their interest for you to earn as much as possible, because their business only grows when yours does. That's why they invest heavily in tools that grow your revenue, rather than just handing you a link and leaving you on your own.
